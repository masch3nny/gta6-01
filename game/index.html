HOW TO PLAY

  • W A S D — move / drive  ·  Shift — sprint  ·  Space — jump / handbrake
  • Mouse — drag to look around, click to shoot (when armed)
  • E — steal / enter a car, exit a car  ·  F — punch  ·  R — reload
  • M — jobs (missions)  ·  Esc / P — pause
  • Crimes raise your wanted level — police cars chase you. Hide to cool off.
  • Finish jobs for Vera to earn cash. Find the 8 hidden green packages.
  • Progress saves automatically to this browser.
